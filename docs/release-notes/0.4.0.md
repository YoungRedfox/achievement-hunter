## Your progress, the moment you connect

**Every game's progress loads right away.** No more "open to check": paste your link and the whole library fills in with unlocked counts and percentages. A 300-game library takes three quick requests instead of three hundred.

**Installed games in the sidebar.** Achievement Hunter now spots the Steam games installed on your PC and lists them under *Library*, with your completion next to each one. One click jumps straight to the game.

**Smaller things**
- Library refresh also refreshes progress.
- Progress only reloads for games you've played since the last check.

Already installed? Click **Download and update** in the sidebar.
