## See what changed after every update

**A "What's new" window after each update.** The first time you open a new version, you get a short summary of what changed, in your language. Skipped a few updates? You'll see all of them, newest first.

**Read it again any time.** Settings → About now has a **What's new** button.

**Settings moved under your avatar.** Click your avatar or name to open your Steam profile.
