## Faster update checks and a proper About page

**Updates show up sooner.** The app now checks every hour and whenever you come back to it, instead of every six hours. There's also a **Check for updates** button in Settings.

**New About section in Settings** with your version number and every link in one place: source code, all releases, problem reports, privacy policy, terms of use, licenses and the Steam Web API terms.

Already installed? Click **Download and update** in the sidebar.
