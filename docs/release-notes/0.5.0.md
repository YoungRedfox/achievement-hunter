## Xbox joins the party

**Connect Xbox next to Steam.** Add your Xbox and PC Game Pass games in Settings with a free OpenXBL key. Progress, missing achievements and step-by-step guides work just like for Steam.

**Platform tabs in your library.** Switch between all platforms, Steam and Xbox, each with its own count.

**See where you own each game.** Every card shows its platform, and if you have the same game on Steam and Xbox, both badges appear.
